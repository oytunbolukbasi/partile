What makes a great housewarming invitation?

The gathering is the easy part — getting everyone there is where Partiful comes in. Create a free housewarming or apartment warming invitation in minutes, send it straight to your guests' phones by text, email, or link, and track RSVPs in real time. No ads, no fees, and most importantly, no group chat chaos. Just a beautiful free invitation and the people you love.

Housewarming party ideas

Whether you're moving into your first apartment or a forever home, a housewarming party is how you make it official! The best ones don't require a big budget or even a fully decorated space. They just need the right people and a reason to show up. Go potluck and have guests bring a dish or a bottle: add a questionnaire to your Partiful invite so everyone can coordinate ahead of time. Build the night around a house tour, with drinks first… and more drinks after. Name a signature cocktail after your street or neighborhood for an easy conversation starter. And don't wait until the place is perfect. The whole point of a housewarming is celebrating the beginning, boxes and bubble wrap included!

How to send free housewarming invitations online

Going from idea to invite takes less than a minute. Easily create an event on partiful.com, no app needed. Pick a theme, then and customize with colors, fonts, and fun animations to match the vibe of your party. Add your date, location, start time, and anything else guests need to know. Then send it by text, link, or email. Guests can RSVP from any browser, no app download required.
